Turning ideas into powerful mobile experiences.

My name is Muzammil Modan. I'm a mobile application developer with over a decade of experience in Android (Java and Kotlin, 7+ years) and Flutter (Dart, 5+ years). I also bring AI into my engineering workflow and into the product features I build.
